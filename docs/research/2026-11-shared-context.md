# shared context artifacts between a human and an AI

research for margin, 2026-11-08. scope: one persistent document both sides read and write that holds the shared understanding of a long project. task/plan chaining and session monitors excluded.

**warrant key.** `read` = fetched this session (URL given). `read (2nd)` = from search-result summaries of secondary sources, not the primary page. `known` = from prior knowledge, not re-fetched this session; treat as inferred and possibly stale.

## bottom line

the strongest sources aren't AI products. they're handoff and decision practices (I-PASS, ADRs, design docs) and two HCI results (Granola's provenance colours, the Lehmann et al. CHI 2026 study). the AI products agree on one mechanism: a small always-loaded index plus on-demand detail, and AI-proposed entries that a human approves. almost none of them handle **staleness**, **ownership of a line**, or **checking that the receiver understood**. margin can win on those three.

## patterns

1. **index always loaded, detail on demand.** Claude Code's MEMORY.md, Devin's pinned vs triggered items, Copilot Spaces' instructions vs sources, and Anthropic's just-in-time references all follow it. margin's one-line status per prompt is already the index; the full pane is the detail.
2. **a hard budget beats a pruning policy.** only Claude Code enforces a size (200 lines, with a reminder near the limit and an error over it). everything else grows until it's ignored. Nygard says it bluntly: "Large documents are never kept up to date."
3. **the AI proposes, the human approves.** Cursor, Devin and NotebookLM's note→source step all gate promotion into durable context. auto-everything (Windsurf, ChatGPT Dreaming) pairs with a readable summary page to win back control.
4. **lines have owners.** Granola's colours, Aider's read-only conventions, Anthropic's "only flip `passes`", and Lehmann's comments-not-edits all say the AI must not silently rewrite what the human wrote. when the AI disagrees, it proposes.
5. **change the status, don't delete.** ADR superseded links, design-doc amendments, append-only lab notebooks and incident logs. history of a decision is part of the context.
6. **staleness is the unsolved problem.** only ChatGPT v3 targets it ("maintaining the correct state over time", 75.1%). Cline names `activeContext` the volatile file but has no mechanism. a stale line is worse than a missing one, because both sides trust it.
7. **handoff needs read-back.** I-PASS's synthesis by receiver, Kilo's "note discrepancies to the user", and the grounding finding that LLMs presume common ground and don't update after repair all point the same way. writing the doc doesn't prove the other side understood it.
8. **negative space is context.** non-goals, alternatives considered, what hasn't worked, dead ends. the products almost never hold it, and the practices almost always do.
9. **shared view is the grounding resource.** Poelitz et al.: a shared view raised success. Dourish: passive shared feedback is cheap awareness. margin's change bars belong to this pattern.

## ideas for margin

1. **ownership marks (Granola; Lehmann et al.; Aider read-only).** render human-written lines and Claude-written lines differently (e.g. bright vs dim). a human edit takes the line over. Claude may not rewrite a human-owned line; it attaches a proposed change the human accepts with a click. this makes "who said this" visible at a glance and stops Claude from quietly drifting the Goal.
2. **supersede, don't delete (Nygard ADRs).** decisions get a status (proposed / accepted / superseded→#n). reversing one leaves a struck entry linked to its replacement. each decision carries a one-line "because" (context) and "costs" (consequences). struck assumptions stay as a trace in the same way.
3. **read-back at handoff (I-PASS synthesis by receiver).** on a new session, or when sulin returns after a gap, Claude posts a 2–3 line "here's where I think we are" into the pane, and sulin confirms or corrects with one action. correcting it is a repair the pane records. this targets the documented failure: LLMs presume common ground and don't update after repair.
4. **last-confirmed stamps and a "still true?" sweep (ChatGPT Dreaming V3; Cline activeContext).** every Goal/Now/assumption line carries when it was last confirmed. lines older than N sessions dim and gather into a "still true?" strip that either side clears. this attacks pattern 6 directly.
5. **a hard budget per section with a nudge (Claude Code MEMORY.md 200-line cap).** e.g. on-the-table ≤ 40 lines, decisions ≤ 12 live. near the cap, margin's status line tells Claude to "merge, supersede or archive"; over it, the write still lands but gets flagged. a budget beats a policy because it forces the pruning conversation.
6. **a severity line (I-PASS illness severity).** one word in the Goal header (steady / watch / stuck) that Claude sets and sulin can override, with a reason. it goes in the per-prompt status line, so every turn knows whether to be cautious.
7. **"learned" and "dead ends" (I-PASS situation awareness; lab notebooks; design-doc alternatives).** the brief asks for "what's been learned", and margin has no home for it. add a short list of findings and of things tried that failed, with why. this is the negative space (pattern 8) that keeps a fresh session from re-trying a dead path.
8. **non-goals in the Goal section (Google design docs).** "not doing" lines that Claude checks before scope grows. it's cheap, and it gives a "suggest refocusing" instruction something concrete to point at.
9. **"used this turn" highlights (Devin Accessed Knowledge; Zhang et al.'s visibility finding).** when Claude relies on a margin item, it cites the item's id and the pane briefly marks it. sulin then sees which context actually drove the turn, and which items never get used (candidates for pruning).
10. **promote out when settled (Windsurf "write it to a Rule"; NotebookLM convert-to-source).** a "settle" action on an on-the-table piece or a decision cluster writes it into the repo (doc, ADR file, CLAUDE.md line) and leaves a link in margin. margin stays the working surface; durable knowledge moves to where it lasts after the project.
11. **legal transitions only for structured parts (Anthropic harness JSON `passes`).** store tasks and decisions as structured records whose only allowed edits are status changes plus appends. free prose stays markdown. that prevents the "model rewrote the list" failure the harness article describes.
12. **a fixed orientation routine (Anthropic harness; Cline "read ALL").** the per-prompt line covers turns. on session start a hook injects the full pane once, in a fixed order (goal, severity, now, open questions, last read-back) rather than whatever happens to fit.

## top picks

ideas 1 (ownership), 2 (supersede), 3 (read-back), and 4+5 together (staleness stamps + budget). they cover the gaps the products leave and that the practices and research identify, and each fits margin's existing sections without a new surface.
